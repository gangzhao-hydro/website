## Research

My research spans mountain flash floods, river floods, and urban inundation, with an emphasis on data-scarce regions and the effects of flood protection under climate change.

I lead JSPS KAKENHI project [24K17353](https://kaken.nii.ac.jp/en/grant/KAKENHI-PROJECT-24K17353/) as PI, developing physics-guided AI for global flood modelling. I am also the lead recipient of [Kurata Grant 1666](https://www.hitachi-zaidan.com/global/topics/topics016.html) on representing levees in global flood models.

## Background

I completed my PhD in Physical Geography at the University of Bristol in 2022, supervised by Professor Paul Bates and Professor Jeff Neal. From May 2022 to March 2024, I was a Project Researcher at the Institute of Industrial Science, The University of Tokyo, working with [Dr. Dai Yamazaki](https://global-hydrodynamics.github.io/) on the CaMa-Flood global hydrodynamic model.

Since April 2024, I have also held a concurrent appointment as an Associate Research Fellow (協力研究員) at the Institute of Industrial Science, The University of Tokyo. My research experience includes collaboration with FATHOM and GAIA Vision Inc. on flood modelling.
